About Garden Planner

Empowering anyone to grow their own food, right in their backyard.

Our Mission

We believe that gardening should be accessible, organized, and deeply rewarding. Whether you have a sprawling backyard or a few pots on a balcony, our mission is to provide the digital tools you need to cultivate a thriving garden.

Why We Built This

Keeping track of planting dates, watering schedules, and fertilization routines can be overwhelming. We built the Home Garden Planner to replace messy notebooks and spreadsheets with a beautifully designed, intuitive platform.

  • 🌱 Visual Planning: Know exactly where every plant is growing.
  • 📅 Smart Schedules: Automated reminders so your plants never go thirsty.
  • 🌿 Plant Library: Rich information on exactly how to care for different species.
